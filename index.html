Our Story

Born from Chhattisgarh's Agricultural Heart

Founded in 2022 in Chirchari, Balod, Chhattisgarh, Gangotri Agrifoods Pvt. Ltd. was built on a simple idea: India's abundant agricultural waste and forestry residue should power India's growth, not pollute it.

We convert locally sourced wood, rice husk, and paddy straw (parali) into premium biomass pellets, serving industrial buyers across India and export markets worldwide.

Our Inputs

Raw Materials We Work With

Every raw material we use is either an agricultural byproduct or sustainably sourced — making our pellets genuinely carbon-neutral and eco-friendly.

🪵

Wood Biomass

Locally sourced wood chips, sawdust, and forest residue from responsibly managed sources in and around Chhattisgarh. Delivers the highest calorific value.

Forest Residues · Chhattisgarh
🌾

Rice Husk

A byproduct of rice milling — massively available across India. Instead of dumping or burning, we pelletize it into consistent, usable fuel for industry.

Rice Mills · CG & surrounding states
🌾

Parali (Paddy Straw)

Post-harvest paddy straw — one of India's biggest sources of open-field burning and air pollution. We procure directly from farmers, giving them a sustainable livelihood alternative.

Farmers · Chhattisgarh & Central India
